About the Project

A careful, non-commercial space for remembrance

This site was built to mark 12 Rabi ul Awwal with a calm, well-sourced digital experience — favoring restraint and accuracy over spectacle, and never depicting Prophet Muhammad ﷺ in any visual form.

Editorial Approach

Historical content on this site draws on classical Seerah and hadith scholarship. Where scholars disagree on dates or details, this site favors widely accepted accounts and avoids presenting contested points as settled fact. Hadith are only included with a stated source, and none are paraphrased in a way that changes their meaning.

Prophet Muhammad ﷺ is never depicted visually — no face, body, silhouette, or AI-generated likeness. Imagery throughout the site is limited to architecture, calligraphy, geometric pattern, and natural scenery.

Sources

  • Qur'an — primary scriptural references, cited by surah and verse.
  • Ibn Hisham, Al-Sirah al-Nabawiyyah — early biographical source, recension of Ibn Ishaq.
  • Al-Tabari, Tarikh al-Rusul wa'l-Muluk — classical historical chronicle.
  • Sahih al-Bukhari & Sahih Muslim — authenticated hadith collections cited throughout the Hadith & Wisdom section.
