A practical compass

Teachings that shape everyday life

The Seerah invites reflection on values that can be lived with humility, care, and consistency.

01

Mercy

Choose gentleness, especially where it is easiest to be harsh.

02

Truthfulness

Let words and actions be trustworthy, clear, and sincere.

03

Justice

Be fair in judgment, including when fairness asks something of us.

04

Patience

Meet difficulty with steadiness, hope, and thoughtful action.

05

Service

Honor people through generosity, gratitude, and useful work.

06

Seeking knowledge

Learn carefully, verify sources, and turn knowledge into character.
